About Beauté Clinic

Beauté Clinic is a medical-led aesthetic and skin clinic in Felinheli, established in 2018 and led by Natasha.

The clinic specialises in undetectable, natural results — treatments that refresh and restore, without changing the face you know.

Nearly a decade of practice and more than 1,400 posts of shared results and education sit behind the clinic's reputation.
