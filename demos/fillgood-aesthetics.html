No myths

Honest answers, before you book

The questions Alicia hears nearly every week — and what is actually true.

“Does filler last forever?”

No. Your body breaks it down gradually — lips usually last six to twelve months, cheeks and chin twelve to eighteen. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something.

“Will I look obvious?”

The results people notice are the overdone ones. When treatment is planned around your face, people tend to tell you that you look well — not ask what you've had done.

“Why the patch test?”

Every laser treatment starts with one, so we know the laser suits your skin before a course begins. It is a safety step, not a sales step.

“Is it just about appearance?”

For many clients it is about self-esteem, confidence and feeling like themselves again. That is taken seriously here — ethical and results-driven means both.
