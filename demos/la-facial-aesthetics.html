Meet Leslie-Anne Halliday

LA Facial Aesthetics is led by Leslie-Anne Halliday — MSc, Registered General Nurse and Independent Nurse Prescriber.

The clinic is registered with Healthcare Improvement Scotland (HIS), so you can be confident you are being treated in a properly regulated medical setting.

Leslie-Anne is known for speaking openly about the business side of aesthetics as well as the clinical side — expect straight answers about what a treatment can and cannot do.
