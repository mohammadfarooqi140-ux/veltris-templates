Meet Yvonne Ward

Purity Aesthetics is owned and run by Yvonne Ward, a nurse prescriber holding a Postgraduate Level 7 qualification in Aesthetic Medicine, with 25 years of clinical experience.

Yvonne specialises in subtle, anti-ageing treatments for clients over 40 — the kind of work that leaves people looking rested and refreshed, never 'done'.

Every treatment begins with a proper consultation, so recommendations are based on your skin, your history and your goals.

From therapeutic masseter treatment to gentle rejuvenation, the approach is the same: less, but better.

Yvonne Ward, Clinic Owner
