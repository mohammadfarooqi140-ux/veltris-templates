Masseter Treatment

No more night guards. No more pain or discomfort.

Constant teeth grinding and clenching enlarges the masseter — the muscle that connects your lower jaw to your cheek — bringing pain, tension and worn teeth. Yvonne has been treating the masseter for two years now, helping clients leave the grinding and the discomfort behind.

Treatments

Keeping the hands of time at bay

Every treatment begins with a medical consultation, and results are checked together at your two-week review.

01

Anti-Wrinkle Injections

Soften the strong muscle movement behind dynamic lines — the wrinkles that appear when you smile or frown — and stop them etching into deep static lines over time. Many of Yvonne's clients have maintained their results with regular treatment since 2020.

Three classic areas from £295
02

Profhilo & Skin Boosters

Bio-stimulating injectable hydration that replaces the hyaluronic acid skin loses with age — surging in moisture while stimulating new collagen production for healthier, firmer skin.

03

Lip Enhancement

Very fine lips are a challenge — and a speciality. With time, patience and the right product and placement, lips are gently enhanced and rejuvenated, softening upper lip lines while keeping everything classy, soft and balanced.

04

Facial Rejuvenation

A considered plan for the whole face rather than a single area — restoring freshness gradually, so you look like yourself on a very good day, never like someone else.
