Meet Sister Stacey Withers

SSW Aesthetics is led by Sister Stacey Withers, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and Prescriber with more than 23 years of clinical nursing experience, and a member of BAMAN (the British Association of Medical Aesthetic Nurses).

That medical background shapes everything: careful assessment, honest advice, and treatments chosen because they are right for you — not because they are fashionable.
